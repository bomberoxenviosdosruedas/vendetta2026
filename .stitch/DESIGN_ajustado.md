## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a tactile, retro-noir aesthetic engineered for a classic browser-style mafia MMORPG. The design balances 1930s prohibition-era underworld grit with late-90s/early-2000s browser gaming nostalgia: heavy burnished bronze bezels, aged parchment ledgers, brass hardware trims, and deep asphalt shadows.

The emotional signature is tense, authoritative, and calculated. Interfaces replicate concrete tactile items—dossiers, rack-mounted instrument gauges, brass-plated ledger books, and criminal ledgers.

Visual language:
- **Tactile / Skeuomorphic Retro:** Pronounced bevels, inset shadow stamping, double-line filigree borders, and subtle parchment textures.
- **Atmospheric Noir Palette:** Deep trenchcoat charcoal, raw cigar-leaf brown, aged legal paper, dried-blood crimson, and phosphor terminal green for real-time timers.
- **Density & Legibility:** Compact information density suited for complex economic simulations, combat logs, syndicate family rosters, and resource telemetry.

## Typography

Typography pairs physical period print with tactical surveillance telemetry:
- **Chivo:** Used for section headers, military operations, crime syndicate titles, and bold card headers. All upper-echelon display headings apply uppercase transform and tight tracking (`0.04em`).
- **Arimo:** Serves as the primary workhorse for mission briefings, dialog, mechanical system instructions, and parchment-based ledger inventories. Clean, unpretentious, and razor-sharp against both ink parchment and dark walnut containers.
- **JetBrains Mono:** Dedicated solely to operational variables, coordinates, bank accounts, cooldown clocks, and unit counts. Enforces strict tabular numeric alignment across leaderboards and stats.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around dense operational cockpits rather than expansive consumer whitespace:
- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop displays run on a 12-column fixed-max layout (`1280px` max-width) centered on `#0a0806`. It splits into a 3-column tactical sidebar (`260px` fixed), a primary ledger workspace (flexible 6–7 columns), and an auxiliary telemetry feed (2–3 columns).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Collapses into a single column. The top instrument bar scrolls horizontally or stacks into a 2x3 telemetry matrix. Side navigation converts to an accordion brass drawer.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** Sidebar docks into icon-and-label tabs; ledger containers collapse horizontal padding to `space-sm`.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** Full multi-panel cockpit presentation with fixed-height scrollable ledger grids.
- **Density Philosophy:** Padding follows an ultra-compact 4px/6px/12px grid rhythm. Zero visual space is wasted; every pixel houses mechanical utility, wood-bevel borders, or status instrumentation.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects airy, soft-cast shadows and glassmorphic blurs in favor of mechanical physical depth, stamped metal plates, carved borders, and chiseled bevels.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep, absorbent `#0a0806`. Unlit background plane.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Wood Casings):** Raised wooden frames (`#1f1813`) bordered with dual high-and-low rims (`1px solid #5a4f3d` top/left, `1px solid #171009` bottom/right). Outer box-shadow: `0 3px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.
- **Level 2 (Parchment Docket Insets):** Inset surfaces stamped into the Level 1 chassis. Border: `1px solid #3a2e24`. Inset shadow: `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`. Background fill: `#eee6d8`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Controls & Buttons):** Classical retro beveled edges (`border-top: 1px solid #7c684d`, `border-left: 1px solid #7c684d`, `border-bottom: 2px solid #1a140d`, `border-right: 2px solid #1a140d`).
- **Level 4 (Modals & Dossiers):** Thick bronze-cast perimeter (`3px solid #4a3a2c`) reinforced with corner bolts, framed by a heavy hard-edge backdrop overlay: `rgba(4, 3, 2, 0.85)`.

## Shapes

The shape system strictly uses industrial zero-radius corners (`0px`). Curves are banned to preserve the vintage industrial/noir silhouette of ledgers, metal file drawers, ammunition boxes, and period print blocks.

- **Buttons & Panels:** Absolute square corners (`0px`).
- **Dividers:** Double-line score (`border-top: 1px solid #332d20; border-bottom: 1px solid #5a4f3d`).
- **Pills / Badges:** Even metric indicators and timer badges retain `0px` or a strictly clipped corner chamfer (`clip-path` 45° 2px cut) rather than soft rounded pill radii, maintaining structural precision.

## Components

### Parchment Container Panel (`v-outer-frame`)
- **Structure:** Dark wood frame outer chassis (`#1f1813`) wrapped in a dual border (`#4a3a2c` outer, `#171009` inner). Inset content bed lined with `#eee6d8`.
- **Inner Ledger:** Text inside is tinted `#241c13` for high-contrast classic paper legibility. Inner borders inside parchment use `#cfc5b0`.

### Bronze Section Header (`v-header-c`)
- **Visuals:** Linear gradient from `#5a4a34` down to `#241b12`. 
- **Typography:** Chivo 13px bold, uppercase, text color `#f2e3c2`, subtle carved drop shadow (`0 -1px 0 #000000`).
- **Trim:** Golden border bottom (`1px solid #dfcca0`).

### Top Resource Bar & Instrument Cells
- **Chassis:** Horizontal dashboard docked at screen top, shaded `#140f0b` with bottom rim `#3a2e24`.
- **Regular Instrument Cell (`vb-cell`):** Background `#1d1712`, 1px solid `#3a2e24`, text `#dfcca0`, metric in JetBrains Mono.
- **Crimson Action Cell (`vb-crimson-cell`):** For critical stats (Health, Vendetta meter, Rage). Gradient `#6d1414` to `#420909`, border `#a02020`, text `#ffffff`.
- **Progress Gauge Bars:** Recessed 6px wells (`#080605`) carrying segmented solid color bars (`#5fe06e` for energy, `#a02020` for blood/health, `#ffe569` for gold).

### Retro Buttons (`retro-btn` & `retro-btn-dark`)
- **Primary MMORPG Button (`retro-btn`):**
  - **Surface:** Gradient `#5a4732` to `#35281b`.
  - **Border:** Raised bevel: top/left `#7c684d`, bottom/right `#140d07`.
  - **Text:** Chivo 12px bold, uppercase, `#ffe569`.
  - **Active State:** Reverse bevel (inset shadow `inset 0 1px 3px #000000`), downward shift 1px.
- **Tactical Dark Button (`retro-btn-dark`):**
  - Surface `#1b1612`, border `#3a2e24`, hover border `#a02020`, text `#dfcca0`.

### Timer Pills (`timer-pill`)
- **Visuals:** Recessed terminal screen (`#050505`) with a 1px border of `#0c7017`.
- **Data Display:** JetBrains Mono 11px bold, phosphor glowing text (`#5fe06e`), faint ambient bloom (`text-shadow: 0 0 4px rgba(95, 224, 110, 0.4)`).

### Dossier Avatar Boxes (`avatar-box`)
- **Role:** Displays Player, Family Don, or Territory Boss portraits.
- **Treatment:** Grayscale or sepia-toned images encased in a heavy `#3a2e24` wood/bronze casing with a 2px inner vignette shadow. Inset nameplate beneath in bronze gradient with rank/family tag in JetBrains Mono.

### Quick-Action Badges (`quick-action-badge`)
- Grid of square action modules (Extort, Bribe, Hit, Smuggle). 
- Deep brown surface (`#1f1813`) with stamped brass icons, crimson badge count on top-right, and heavy mechanical press states when triggered.

### Scoreboards & Ledger Tables
- **Header:** `v-header-c` styling across column groups.
- **Borders:** Crisp, 1px solid black (`#000000`) or deep timber (`#332d20`) dividing lines.
- **Striping:** Alternating parchment tracks (`#eee6d8` and `#e4dbca`). Selected or player-owned row illuminates in soft brass gold (`#ffe569` at 25% tint over parchment).
- **Numbers:** Tabular figures aligned right using JetBrains Mono.

### Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Recessed parchment box (`#f4eee2`), inner shadow `inset 0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.35)`, text `#241c13`, focus rim `1px solid #a02020`.
- **Checkboxes:** Square brass bevel checkbox (`14x14px`), unchecked `#120e0a`, checked reveals a crisp blood-crimson `#a02020` block checkmark.
